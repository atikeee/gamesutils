Reading Signals

Type names the indicator. Signal is its rule-based interpretation. Value is the measurement, not a confidence score. Basis explains the rule used.

Labels and Overall Bias

Buy watch / Sell watch
Bullish or bearish conditions worth watching, not instructions to trade. Neutral means neither directional rule is met. Context means background information, excluded from the technical bias.
Overall bias
Buy watch requires at least two BUY signals and more BUY than SELL signals; Sell watch uses the reverse rule. Otherwise the result is Mixed / Neutral. For example, three BUY and one SELL gives Buy watch; two of each gives Mixed / Neutral. This is a count of rules, not a probability of profit.

Values and Examples

Trend: SMA20 / SMA50
SMA20 and SMA50 are average daily closing prices over 20 and 50 days, in the stock's price units. SMA20 105.00 / SMA50 100.00 means the recent average is higher. With current price above 105, this is bullish. Price below SMA20 with SMA20 below SMA50 is bearish; other combinations are neutral. A new crossover means the averages have just changed order.
RSI: Relative Strength Index
A 14-day momentum reading from 0 to 100. 25 is below 30: an oversold / Buy watch. 75 is above 70: an overbought / Sell watch. Readings from 30 through 70 are neutral. Oversold does not mean price must rise, nor overbought that it must fall. Undefined (flat price) means there was no price variation and is treated as neutral.
MACD: Moving Average Convergence Divergence
The value is the MACD histogram: the MACD line minus its signal line. +0.450 indicates bullish momentum; -0.450 indicates bearish momentum; zero is neutral. It is not a percentage. Its size depends on the stock's price scale, so values are not directly comparable between stocks.
20-Day Range
95.00 - 110.00 shows the lowest and highest closing prices in the previous 20 daily bars. Current price above 110 flags an upward breakout / Buy watch; below 95 flags a downward breakout / Sell watch; inside the range or on its boundary is neutral. This uses closes, not intraday highs and lows.
Volume
The latest daily-bar volume divided by the previous 20-bar average. 1.00x means normal activity, 2.00x twice the average, and 0.50x half the average. At least 1.50x highlights higher activity, not a buy or sell direction. Intraday volume can be incomplete. Unavailable means the average volume could not support a valid ratio.
Headlines
3 positive / 1 negative matches counts headlines containing positive or negative keywords, not the number of words or a sentiment percentage. A headline can match both groups. Context and negation can be missed; these counts do not affect the technical bias.

At least 60 daily bars are required. Signals can conflict, and market data may be delayed. These experimental rules are not investment advice or a guarantee of returns.
